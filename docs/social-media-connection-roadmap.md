# Social Media Connection — Next Phase Roadmap

Engineering follow-up after the live Facebook Lead Ads path. Use this when prioritising the next investment; do not treat these items as shipped.

## Priority order

1. **Distinct Instagram attribution**  
   When a Lead Ad originates from Instagram Instant Forms, store platform/source as Instagram instead of always Facebook / Meta.

2. **Historical lead backfill** — **Done for recent forms**  
   Sync Now / connect pulls recent Lead Ad form submissions (per-form limit) into CRM. Expand limits or date windows if needed.

3. **Page (and later form) picker**  
   Let the customer choose which Facebook Pages to connect instead of linking every Page returned by Meta. Later: optional Lead Form selection.

4. **Google Ads completion**  
   Customer Ads account selection, campaign/spend metrics sync into Marketing Campaigns, and Google lead form ingest if required.

5. **Unify Meta connect/disconnect**  
   Settings → CRM Meta card and Marketing → Connected Apps should create and remove the same connection records so disconnect never leaves orphan rows.

## Out of scope for this module

- Social posting, DM inbox, or WhatsApp messaging UI (separate product surfaces)
- Fabricated calling / WhatsApp conversation demos elsewhere in the org app
